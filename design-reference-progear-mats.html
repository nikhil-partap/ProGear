( 03 ) — The Process

Poured, pressed, cured.

01 / 04

STEP 01

Raw compound

Recovered rubber crumb and virgin TPE are weighed to the gram, then blended with a mineral binder chosen for one property: memory. The mix that leaves this stage decides how the floor forgives.

STEP 02

Compression

1,200 tonnes of pressure compress the blend into a monolithic slab. No foam, no air pockets — density is chased, not poured.

STEP 03

Cure & rest

Seventy-two hours under controlled heat. The slab is rested longer than the schedule allows, because tolerances are set in stillness.

STEP 04

Tolerance pass

Every surface is measured for thickness, flatness and grip. Anything beyond ±0.4 mm never sees a gym floor.
